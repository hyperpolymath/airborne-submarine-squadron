== 1. Market Reality and Comparable Archetypes

The goal to turn Airborne Submarine Squadron into a multi-million-dollar commercial cult hit must be grounded in contemporary indie games market data (2025–2026):

* **The Brutal Median:** Across 15,000+ annual releases on Steam, the median gross revenue for indie games is ~$249. Over 65% of releases earn less than $1,000 lifetime.
* **The Breakout Ceiling:** The top 0.5% of indie games achieve over $1,000,000 in gross revenue. These are almost exclusively games with extreme mechanical depth, systemic replayability, emergent comedy, or massive influencer spectator value.
* **The Target Precedent — Vlambeer's LUFTRAUSERS (2014):**
  - **Mechanics:** 2D arcade aerial combat with momentum-based physics, distinct weapon/hull/engine combinations, high difficulty, and instant restarts.
  - **Commercial Performance:** Recouped development costs within 72 hours; achieved lifetime sales of 411,000+ units on Steam ($9.99 base price, generating ~$3.5M+ gross revenue).
* **The Contemporary Opportunity:** ASS builds directly on the Sopwith and Luftrausers lineage, but expands the single-plane flight loop into three seamless dimensions: **Atmospheric Dogfighting**, **Deep Ocean Submarine Stealth** (thermal layers, sonar, caterpillar drive), and **Orbital Spaceflight** (solar physics, planet destinations).

== 2. Four-Stage Commercialization Strategy

=== Stage 1: Zero-Friction Web Proving Ground (Months 1–3)
* **Platforms:** itch.io (Pay-What-You-Want, 90% rev share), CrazyGames, and Poki.
* **Distribution Asset:** Single-click browser play via static HTML5/WASM with instant loading (<6 MB footprint).
* **Objective:** Gather qualitative telemetry, refine flight control feel, and build initial viral momentum without store barriers.
* **Target:** 100,000+ web plays, build community discord/newsletter, validate core game loop retention (>6 minutes average session length).

=== Stage 2: Cult Branding and Influencer Flywheel (Months 3–6)
* **The Double-Entendre Advantage ("Airborne Submarine Squadron" / "ASS"):**
  - *Recommendation:* Lean aggressively into the meme and acronym in community channels, merchandise, and gameplay dialogue, while maintaining "Airborne Submarine Squadron" as the clean store title to prevent algorithmic filtering.
  - *Juice and Spectacle:* Polish visual punch—screen shake, particle plumes, water surface hedgehop spray, and dramatic death blurbs.
  - *Daily Seeded Runs:* Leverage deterministic simulation to generate a daily challenge (`?seed=YYYY-MM-DD`) with global leaderboards, driving daily Twitch/YouTube streams.

=== Stage 3: The Multi-Role Co-Op Expansion (Months 6–9)
* **The "Lover's in a Dangerous Spacetime" / "Barotrauma" Factor:**
  - Transition from solo flight to 4-player shared submarine co-op (Pilot, Gunner, Engineer, Commander).
  - Emergent chaos of multi-crew management under attack generates high viral streamability.
  - Integrated via WebRTC DataChannel (low-latency input replication) and Burble spatial voice.

=== Stage 4: Premium PC / Console Launch (Months 9–12)
* **Storefronts:** Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Nintendo Switch.
* **Pricing Tier:** $9.99 base price ($14.99 Deluxe Edition with digital soundtrack and retro DOS artbook).
* **Launch Requirements:**
  - Minimum 25,000 pre-launch Steam wishlists to ensure algorithmic rotation into "New & Trending" (converting at 0.15x = 3,750 units week 1 = ~$37,500).
  - Relicensed to MPL-2.0 or dual-licensed to allow clean linking against the proprietary Steamworks SDK.
  - Dedicated desktop container via Gossamer native shell (WebKitGTK/WebView2/Cocoa) with zero electron/tauri bloat.
